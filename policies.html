BOOKING POLICIES

Please review our appointment terms and conditions before requesting a service. These policies help Only1Zeke provide professional, reliable and quality barber services to every customer.

01 — Booking & Appointment Confirmation

  • All appointment requests are subject to barber availability and approval.
  • A 50% deposit of the total requested service cost is required to confirm an appointment.
  • The remaining balance is due at the appointment.
  • Submitting an appointment request does not automatically confirm your appointment.
  • Customers must provide accurate contact information and requested service details.

02 — Cancellation & Deposit Refunds

Customers must request cancellation within 24 hours of making their confirmed booking to qualify for a deposit refund.

  • Cancellation must occur before the scheduled appointment begins.
  • Cancellations requested after the 24-hour booking window will result in forfeiture of the original deposit.
  • Missed appointments do not qualify for a deposit refund.
  • Customers should contact Only1Zeke directly to request cancellation and refund review.

The 24-hour refund window begins when your appointment is confirmed, not 24 hours before your scheduled appointment.

03 — Late Arrival Policy

Only1Zeke provides a 10-minute grace period for scheduled appointments.

  • Customers are expected to arrive on time.
  • Customers arriving more than 10 minutes late may have their appointment canceled.
  • Late arrivals resulting in cancellation may result in forfeiture of the booking deposit.
  • Please contact the barber immediately if you anticipate arriving late.

04 — No-Show Policy

A no-show occurs when a customer fails to attend a confirmed appointment without canceling before the appointment begins.

  • The original booking deposit will be forfeited.
  • Customers who fail to attend a confirmed appointment will be restricted from making future bookings with Only1Zeke.

NO-SHOW CUSTOMERS WILL LOSE FUTURE BOOKING PRIVILEGES.

05 — Customer Rescheduling

Free rescheduling is not available when a customer requests to change a confirmed appointment.

  • The original booking deposit will be forfeited when the customer chooses to reschedule.
  • A new 50% booking deposit is required to confirm the replacement appointment.
  • The new appointment is subject to barber availability and approval.
  • The new deposit will be credited toward the replacement appointment's service total.

This rescheduling policy is separate from the eligible cancellation refund policy.

06 — Same-Day Appointments

Same-day appointments are subject to availability and an additional $20.00 surcharge per appointment.

The surcharge is added to the service total before calculating the required 50% deposit.

07 — Barber Cancellation

If Only1Zeke cancels a confirmed appointment, the customer may choose either:

  • A full refund of the deposit paid for the canceled appointment.
  • Free rescheduling with the original deposit transferred to the replacement appointment.

No additional rescheduling fee or replacement deposit is required for a barber-initiated cancellation when the same service is rebooked.

08 — Mobile House-Call Services

House-call services originate from Buffalo, New York.

  • Adult house calls start at $60.00.
  • Kids house calls start at $40.00.
  • Single-client house-call prices vary based on one-way travel distance.
  • Single-client appointments over 45 miles require a custom quote.
  • Customers must provide an accurate service address.
  • Two or more clients receiving house-call services at the same location qualify for waived travel fees.

09 — Accepted Payments

Only1Zeke accepts the following payment methods:

  • Square Tap to Pay
  • Credit cards
  • Debit cards
  • Cash

Online deposit checkout is currently being prepared. Contact Only1Zeke for deposit payment instructions.

Never submit credit or debit card information through the appointment request form.

10 — Customer Agreement

Customers are expected to review and acknowledge these booking policies before requesting an appointment.

These policies apply subject to applicable consumer protection laws and do not limit any rights or remedies provided by law.

Any changes to booking policies will apply to future bookings and will not retroactively change the agreed terms of an existing confirmed appointment.
